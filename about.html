Our story

Curtains and décor, done properly

Gilly Interiors (also known as Gilly Curtains & Decor) started with a simple idea: beautiful interiors should be easy to order and perfectly fitted. Today we stitch and deliver curtains, supply and install rods, carpets, wallpapers and turf grass, and stock décor pieces for homes, offices and shops.

  • Based at Kaiyoni Building, opposite Litein High Gate
  • Serving Litein, Kericho County and Nairobi
  • 8,000+ followers and growing on Facebook
